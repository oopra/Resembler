Resembler

Whose eyes? Whose nose? Whose stubborn chin? Put the photos side by side and find out — feature by feature.

1 The little one

One clear, front-on photo. Looking straight at the camera, face not too small in the frame — that is the whole trick to a good answer.

2 Who to compare against

Mum, Dad, a grandparent, an uncle — up to six. Their names never leave this device: the photos are sent as unlabelled A, B, C, precisely so nobody gets credit for being called “Dad”.

3 Compare

How carefully?

Each look shuffles the running order and hides the names. Where the looks disagree, the result says so instead of picking a winner.

✦ The verdict

Feature by feature

For fun, and only for fun. This compares what two faces look like in two photographs. It is not a paternity test, not a DNA test and not face recognition — it cannot tell you who is related to whom, and a different pair of photos of the same two people can give a different answer. Children also change enormously as they grow; the toddler who is “all Dad” is frequently a teenager who is all Mum.

Photos are cropped and prepared on your device, sent for a single comparison, and not stored — not by this app, not in your browser. Names and relationships are never sent at all.
